Welcome

Plan delivery routes for a fleet of drones in three steps:

  1. Choose how many orders and drones you have (and where the depot is).
  2. Click Plan Routes — random delivery orders are created around the depot.
  3. Review which drone delivers what, the route map, distances and times.

How it works: orders are split between drones by angle around the depot, then each drone's shortest round trip is found with Dynamic Programming (exact for up to 12 orders per drone) or a Nearest Neighbour + 2-opt heuristic for larger groups. An optional battery range makes the planner skip orders a drone cannot reach and warn you about them.
